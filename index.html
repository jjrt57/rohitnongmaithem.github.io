About

Bridging Theory & Experiment

I'm an M.Sc. Chemistry candidate at Manipur University specialising in Physical & Computational Chemistry, with hands-on experience in quantum chemistry, density functional theory, and molecular spectroscopy.

My research sits at the intersection of experiment and simulation — I've processed photoluminescence and UV-Vis data in the lab, then cross-validated those results against DFT predictions. When they don't match, I dig until I know why.

I also build tools to make computational workflows faster and less error-prone, and I document the undocumented — the cryptic HPC errors and edge cases that cost researchers days of debugging.

I'm actively seeking research, RA, or junior computational roles in materials science, spectroscopy, or ML-for-chemistry. Open to remote, relocation, or internship formats.

Gaussian Log Parser

A Streamlit web app for parsing Gaussian .log and .out files. Automatically extracts final SCF energies, HOMO/LUMO values, and optimised geometry — then renders the molecule as an interactive 3D viewer. Built because reading raw Gaussian output by hand wastes hours.

QE-2-CIF

A Python CLI tool that converts Quantum ESPRESSO DFT output files into standard Crystallographic Information Files (.cif). Uses pymatgen to isolate the final relaxed geometry and export it directly into VESTA, Mercury, or Materials Project — removing a copy-paste step that introduced coordinate errors in post-processing.
